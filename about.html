Who we are

One discipline, applied to our ground and yours

A Nairobi-based mining and mineral resources advisory company, built on a single idea: know what the rock actually holds before you move or value it.

The name

Why "in situ"

Insitu Resources Advisory Ltd takes its name from in situ, the geologist's term for a resource evaluated in place, before it is moved or assumed.

That is the standard we hold to on every asset we take on and every report we sign: what the ground actually supports, documented so anyone can check it. It is the same discipline whether the asset is ours or a client's.
