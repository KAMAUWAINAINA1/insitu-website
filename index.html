Pillar 01 — Mining

Building a mineral portfolio, the right way

Insitu holds and pursues its own mineral interests. We are an operator in our own right, growing a portfolio of exploration and development-stage assets across the region.

We are early stage and deliberately so. The portfolio is being built asset by asset, each one taken on only when the geology and the economics justify it. This page describes how we work, not a claim of production.

01

Acquisition & licences

Securing prospective ground and mineral rights through disciplined, well-researched acquisition rather than speculation.

02

Exploration & development

Advancing early-stage assets with the same estimation and QA/QC standards we hold our advisory clients to.

03

Partnerships & joint ventures

Bringing in the right operators, capital, and landholders to move projects forward on fair, transparent terms.

04

Responsible operations

Building environmental, social, and community responsibility into each asset from the first licence, not as an afterthought.

Pillar 02 — Advisory

Technical advisory across the resource lifecycle

The same expertise we use on our own ground, offered independently to explorers, miners, and investors who need defensible answers about theirs.

01

Resource estimation

Independent mineral resource models built and reported to recognised codes, ready for boards, financiers, and regulators.

  • Geological and grade modelling
  • JORC and NI 43-101 aligned reporting
  • Resource classification and audits
02

Exploration geology

Turning geology into a drill plan. We help you spend exploration budgets where the rock says it counts.

  • Target generation and ranking
  • Mapping, sampling, and QA/QC design
  • Drill programme planning and supervision
03

Technical due diligence

Clear-eyed technical review for investors, lenders, and acquirers weighing a mineral asset or project.

  • Independent project and data review
  • Valuation input and risk assessment
  • Second opinions on prior work
04

Compliance & ESG

Keeping projects permitted and defensible, from licence applications through environmental and social obligations.

  • Permitting and licence support
  • Environmental and social advisory
  • Regulatory and reporting readiness

Reporting standards we work to

  • JORC
  • NI 43-101
  • SAMREC
  • CRIRSCO

Who we are

One discipline, applied to our ground and yours

Insitu Resources Advisory Ltd is a Nairobi-based mining and mineral resources advisory company. We build our own portfolio of mineral assets and, in parallel, advise explorers, miners, and investors on theirs.

Our name says it plainly. In situ is the resource evaluated in place, before it is moved or assumed. That is the standard we hold to on every asset we take on and every report we sign: what the ground actually supports, documented so anyone can check it.
